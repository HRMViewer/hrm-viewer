1Getting started

  1. Download HRM-Viewer-<version>-full.zip from github.com/HRMViewer/hrm-viewer/releases and unzip the HRM Viewer folder somewhere permanent, for example Documents\HRM Viewer.
  2. Double-click HRM Viewer.exe. It opens in its own window. The first time, it offers to add shortcuts to your desktop and Start menu.
    • Nothing else needs installing: everything it uses, including its own Python, is in the folder.
    • If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Click Load new database (top right), paste the path to your game's database.db (or choose the file) and press Load.
Tip: you can also drag database.db onto HRM Viewer.exe to open the viewer and load it in one go.

3Loading your saves

Load a newer save

Save in the game, then click Load new database and press Load (the path is remembered). Each load builds on what's stored:

  • Records in the new file replace older versions; history the game has since deleted (old results, finances, news…) is kept.
  • If a file is older than what's stored, or from a different career, the viewer asks first.

Automatic updates

Settings → Automatic updates: switch it on and leave the viewer open. Every time you save in the game, the viewer loads the new file by itself. ● Auto at the top shows it's on. You can change which file it watches there too.

Several databases

Settings → Databases lets you keep different saves apart, switch between them, rename or delete them, and bring in data from another HRM Viewer folder. Click the database name at the top right to get there quickly.

Each database follows one player. If a save's player is different from the database's (for example your heir has taken over), or the file is from a different save, the viewer asks before loading it, even during automatic updates: Same world keeps using this database, or make a new database for the new player. The Databases list shows each database's player and any players it followed before.

4Horses

  • Horse page — the form guide (record by track, condition and distance), race record with trainer, stable and gear (point at the gear letters for full names), finishing-position and value charts, abilities, health, offspring, awards and track records held.
  • Horse awards — the Awards tab on a horse's page lists every season award and Group/Listed race win, with the jockey and the stable it raced for. Filter by season awards or race wins, wins only, year, or search.
  • Photos — on a horse's page click Add photos (or Gallery) to upload pictures (JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP; click to choose or drag them in). Under each photo, ★ Profile makes it the horse's profile picture (next to its name, and small in All horses and the Horse table) and ▭ Banner puts it across the top of the horse's hover card (no banner chosen = no picture on the hover card). Under Banner view you choose how it looks: drag the preview to pick which part shows, zoom, fill the banner or show the whole picture, short/medium/tall, and sharp pixels for pixel art. Add as many photos as you like; the row slides sideways (‹ › or scroll) when there are more than fit. Photos are kept with that database, on your computer.
  • Family tree — four generations on the page; bigger trees (8, 12, 16 generations) as a list with copy buttons. Tick a name under Most influential ancestors to highlight it everywhere in the tree.
  • All horses — search, filter (alive/deceased, sex) and sort every horse.
  • Horse table — every horse with its key stats in one sortable table.
  • Top sires / Top dams / Broodmare sires — ranked by how their foals have done; filter by foal age, sex and minimum runners.

5Racing & racecourses

  • Meetings and Upcoming meetings — every race day. On a meeting, click ▸ next to a runner to open its form: stats for these conditions, jockey and trainer records, and its last starts.
  • Race results — the full result, prize money and sectionals for every runner (with the fastest of each section and top speeds).
  • All races / Group 1 results / Upcoming Group 1s — find any race; filter by type and date.
  • Racecourses — each course shows its track records (click a distance to see every earlier holder), sectional records, tracks, tickets, recent and upcoming meetings, and feature races.

6Stables

Pick one of your stables from the drop-down in the side menu, or use the ◀ ▶ bar on a stable page to step through every stable you manage, own or share in. Stables you manage come first, marked Manager (or Owner & manager). Each stable page has four tabs:

TabWhat's on it
OverviewCash, net worth, horses, staff, all-time race record, shareholders and recent results.
FinancialsIncome and expenses year by year (▲▼ change on the year before), both broken down by category; Month by month: each month's income, expenses and net for the year you pick, with ▸ for that month's categories; and the payment detail, where Expenses · Income switches between every payment out and every payment in, grouped by category and type, with a filter box. Net worth, cash and income charts are here too.
Horse financesEvery horse the stable has had, now and in the past, with what it earned and cost while it was there, including what was paid to buy it (at auction or privately, or by one of the stable's group stables before it was passed on) and what it sold for. Click ▸ on a horse for its breakdown and year-by-year figures.
AwardsEvery award tied to the stable: its own awards, the season awards and Group/Listed race wins of its horses while they were there, and its staff's awards. Filter by kind, wins only, year, or search by name.

Every race (top right of a stable) lists every run the stable has had, with filters and Export CSV.

7People & awards

  • People — jockeys, trainers, managers and staff. A person's page shows their rides, skills, personality, jobs and family. Its Awards tab lists their own awards and every Group/Listed race win they rode, trained, managed or looked after, with a filter for each role.
  • Family tree — a person's Family tree tab shows their ancestors up to 8 generations back (four as a chart, all eight as a fold-out list) and every relative the save links to them: spouses, parents, brothers and sisters, children, aunts and uncles, nieces and nephews, and cousins of any degree (e.g. second cousin once removed). Switch between Close family · + Aunts, uncles & cousins · Everyone. The game links parents and children born since your career began, so trees grow as generations pass.
  • Dynasty — the <surname> dynasty tab shows the family that shares their last name: its earliest known member, a tree generation by generation (♥ = married to), every member and those who married in, the stables it owns, its total wins and awards, and its births, marriages, divorces and deaths.
  • Jockey stats (People side menu) — everyone's jockey, trainer and stable numbers side by side: rides, wins, strike rate and prize money as a jockey and as a trainer, plus the stable they work at now, its wins and prize money, and what they've earned as a manager. Switch between Jockeys · Trainers · Stable managers · Everyone and click any heading to sort.
  • Awards — season award winners year by year (Horse of the Year, age and distance divisions, breeding, people, stables, Hall of Fame), or Most awards for the all-time list.

8Tables & charts

Tables

  • Sort: click any column heading (click again to reverse).
  • Filter rows: bigger tables have a filter box next to their CSV button. Esc clears it.
  • CSV: downloads the table for Excel. On list pages, Export CSV at the top downloads every matching row, not just the page.
  • Rows per page: change it under a list (up to 5,000), or set a default in Settings.

Charts

  • Point at a chart for exact values on that date.
  • Linear / Log switches the scale; Log stops one huge value squashing the rest.
  • Time range buttons (All, 10Y … 1M), ⤢ to open the chart large, PNG for a picture, CSV for the numbers.
  • Click a name in the legend to hide that line; double-click it to show only that line.

10Updating the viewer

From GitHub (easiest)

New versions are published on GitHub. When one is out, the viewer asks when it opens and shows what's new: click Install now and it downloads, installs and restarts by itself. Remind me later asks again next time (a bar at the top stays until then); Skip this version stops asking about that one.

Settings → Viewer → Updates shows whether you're up to date and has Check now. All versions lists every published version with its changes: Go back to this installs an earlier one if you don't like a new version, and you can update again from the same list whenever you like.

Untick Check for updates automatically there if you'd rather the viewer never looks for new versions by itself; Check now still works whenever you press it.

Anyone can download the full viewer from github.com/HRMViewer/hrm-viewer/releases.

From a file

  1. Updates also come as HRM-Viewer-update-<version>.zip. Don't unzip it.
  2. Go to Settings and click Install an update, choose the file and click Install.
  3. The viewer restarts on the new version by itself.

Updates never touch your stored data or settings. The previous version is kept, and Versions & changes can put it back. A full download (HRM-Viewer-<version>-full.zip) is a complete fresh copy; to keep your data, copy your old data folder into it.

11Good to know

  • Your data stays on your computer in the data folder. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Sectional times are only kept by the game for Group races and for roughly the last few months of other races, so some racecourses have track records but no sectional records.
  • Live data: the game only writes to database.db when you save, so the viewer shows your last save.
  • All tables (Settings → All tables) shows every table in the save exactly as stored; type under a column and press Enter to filter (e.g. >100, !null).
  • If the window opens in your browser instead, Windows is missing the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime; the viewer tells you where to get it.
